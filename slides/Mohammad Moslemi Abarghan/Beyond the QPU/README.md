# Beyond the QPU: Writing Linux Drivers for Real Quantum Hardware

<p dir="ltr" align="left"><strong>Presenter:</strong> Mohammad Moslemi Abarghan</p>

**Session:** Talk

## Abstract

Quantum computing is usually discussed through algorithms and quantum programming, but practical quantum systems also require a strong systems-software layer. This talk explores what it takes to bring a Quantum Processing Unit (QPU) into the Linux device model, focusing on PCIe, MMIO, DMA, interrupts, and kernel-driver design. Using a virtual QPU implemented as a QEMU PCI device, we will walk through the architecture of a Linux QPU driver and its interaction with a quantum simulation backend. The talk connects familiar concepts from GPU, FPGA, and high-performance networking drivers with the unique challenges of building infrastructure for quantum hardware.
