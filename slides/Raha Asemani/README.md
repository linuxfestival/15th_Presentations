# Beyond the Model: Engineering AI Systems for the Real World

<p dir="ltr" align="left"><strong>Presenter:</strong> Raha Asemani</p>

**Session:** Talk

## Abstract

AI is no longer just about building better models. From LLM-powered applications to agentic systems, turning AI capabilities into real-world impact requires robust architectures, scalable infrastructure, and thoughtful engineering across the entire AI stack.

This talk explores what happens beyond the model: how modern AI systems are designed, deployed, and scaled, from GPU infrastructure and LLM serving to RAG and agentic architectures. We will examine the engineering challenges behind building AI systems that are not only intelligent, but reliable, scalable, and capable of operating in real-world environments.

Prerequisites: Basic familiarity with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning concepts. No prior experience with specific frameworks or AI infrastructure is required.
