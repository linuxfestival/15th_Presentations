# WebRTC for Those Who Know TCP/UDP

<p dir="ltr" align="left"><strong>Presenters:</strong> Mahdi Haghparast, Sina Shabani</p>

**Session:** Talk

## Abstract

WebRTC is the technology behind many modern real-time audio and video applications, but it can seem complex if you don't know where to start. In this talk, we will build an intuitive understanding of WebRTC from the networking concepts developers already know, especially TCP and UDP. We’ll explore how WebRTC establishes connections, the role of ICE, STUN, and TURN, and how audio and video are transported between peers. The goal is to give beginners a clear mental model of what happens under the hood when a WebRTC call is made.

Prerequisites: Basic understanding of computer networking, especially TCP/IP and UDP. No prior WebRTC experience is required.
