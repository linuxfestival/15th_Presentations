# Open Telemetry

<p dir="ltr" align="left"><strong>Presenter:</strong> Farshad Nickfetrat</p>

**Session:** Talk

## Abstract

OpenTelemetry: From Data Collection to Understanding System Behavior
In the world of distributed systems and microservices architectures, identifying the root cause of problems and performance bottlenecks is not always straightforward.
In this talk, we will explore the concept of observability and the role of OpenTelemetry in monitoring and troubleshooting systems. We will also examine how the three core signals—logs, metrics, and traces—can provide a more comprehensive view of service behavior.
We will also look at the OpenTelemetry architecture, how telemetry data is collected and transported, and how it is used in modern infrastructure and Kubernetes environments.
